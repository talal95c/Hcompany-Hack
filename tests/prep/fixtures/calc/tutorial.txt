Hi everyone, welcome back to the channel! Today a quick spreadsheet tip with a small monthly sales table.
First, let's total the Sales column: click cell B6, right below the numbers, type =SUM(B2:B5) and press Enter.
Next, make the header stand out: select cells A1 and B1, then click the Bold button on the formatting toolbar.
Finally, add a chart: select the range A1:B5, open the Insert menu, choose Chart, and click Finish in the wizard.
That's it, your table is ready. See you next time!
